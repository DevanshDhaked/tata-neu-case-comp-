## Company Introduction

Tata Neu is India's ambitious "Super App," designed to bring a massive ecosystem of diverse consumer brands under one digital roof. From fashion and electronics to groceries and travel, the app aims to be the single entry point for an Indian consumer's digital life, powered by a unified reward currency called "NeuCoins."

## The Problem Statement

While Tata Neu has successfully acquired millions of users, it faces a significant challenge with **"Engagement Frequency"**, specifically among the Gen Z / Student demographic.

For a typical college student, the app suffers from the **"Buy and Bye"** syndrome.

- **The "Event-Based" Usage:** Students open the app only when they have a specific, high-ticket need (e.g., buying a smartphone during a sale or booking a flight home). Once the transaction is over, the app lies dormant on their phone for months.
- **The "Standalone" Preference:** For lower-ticket, high-frequency categories (like fashion or grocery), students often prefer using the standalone, brand-specific apps rather than the "Super App," as the native experience feels faster and more familiar.
- **The Missing "Hook":** Unlike Instagram (Social) or Cred (Curiosity), Tata Neu currently lacks a compelling reason for a user to open the app *without* a purchase intent. There is no "Daily Loop."

## Your Challenge

You are a **Product Manager** at Tata Neu focused on the "Youth & Gen Z" segment.

Your mandate is to introduce a **new engagement vertical or feature** that transforms Tata Neu from a "Shopping Mall" (visit rarely) into a "Daily Habit" (visit often). You need to give a college student a reason to open the app at least **2-3 times a week**, even if they aren't buying anything.

**Analyze the current market landscape and identify ONE core gap in the student user journey.**

**Your proposal must include:**

- **The Diagnosis:** Why does the current app fail to hold the attention of a 20-year-old? (Is it too serious? Too cluttered? Lack of content?)
- **The Solution:** Propose **one high-impact feature** (e.g., A content feed? A gamified streak? A student-exclusive club? A utility tool?) that drives frequent opens.
- **The Journey:** Describe how this feature fits into a student's daily or weekly routine.
- **The Metrics:** Define the primary success metric (e.g., *Increase in Weekly Active Users (WAU) by 15%*).

*Note: You are free to leverage any brand within the Tata ecosystem, but the solution must be centered within the Tata Neu app interface.*
