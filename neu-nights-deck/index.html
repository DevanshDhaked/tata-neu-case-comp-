04The Metrics · Proving a new habit

Success isn't another download. It's another day they return.

PRIMARY METRIC
Weekly habit rate

The % of student users who open Tata Neu on at least 3 distinct days in a week.

PROPOSED TARGET
+20%

relative lift in weekly habit rate within a 12-week campus pilot, vs a matched control group.

A target to test, not a forecast. Baseline is measured at pilot start; none is assumed here.
