## 2. Feature rules (agreed in `neu-nights.md`)

- 3 merchant payments of ₹10+ on Tata Neu UPI = 1 token per day. Friend and bank transfers don't count.
- Play window 9 pm – 12 am, one play per token.
- Reward 0.5%–2% of the day's qualifying spend, as NeuCoins. Eligible spend capped at ₹700/day.
- Rare prizes: Starbucks drink (~₹250, 1 in 500), 2,000 NeuCoins or ₹2,000 Croma voucher (1 in 5,000).

## 3. Modeled numbers (estimates, not operating data)

| Number | How it's built |
|---|---|
| ~0.84% average reward | Example odds: 0.5% on 50% of nights, 1% on 35%, 1.5% on 12%, 2% on 3%. Tata can tune these. |
| ₹33.60 / month everyday rewards | ₹200/day qualifying spend × 0.84% × 20 token nights |
| ₹18 / month rare prizes | ≈ ₹0.90 per player per night × 20 nights |
| **~₹52 / active student / month** | Sum of the two lines above (≈ ₹619 / year). Face value; real cost is lower (coin expiry, cost-of-goods redemption, brand-funded coupons). |
| "1% of ₹200 = ₹2" | One illustrative nightly outcome used on slides 3 and 5. |

## 4. Proposed target (our choice, not a forecast)

- **Primary metric:** weekly habit rate = % of student users who open Tata Neu on ≥ 3 distinct days in a week.
- **Proposed target:** +20% relative lift in weekly habit rate within a 12-week campus pilot vs matched control campuses.
- The source proposal left the target open (relative lift vs pilot framing). +20% was set for the deck as a testable bar. No baseline is assumed; it is measured at pilot start.
- Supporting: week-1 token activation, week-4 retention and nights played, Tata Neu UPI payments per student per week, coin redemption.
- Guardrails: cost within ~₹52 per student/month, UPI success rate and load time, gaming (e.g. clusters of ₹10 payments to one shop).
