## 3. THE CORE PRODUCT CONCEPT

Our feature is called **NEU NIGHTS**.

The key idea:

**"Pay during the day. Play at night. Earn with Tata Neu."**

The mechanics are defined in `neu-nights.md`:

- Students use Tata Neu UPI for their normal everyday payments.
- Three qualifying merchant payments of at least ₹10 unlock one nightly token.
- Between 9 PM and 12 AM, students can open Tata Neu and use the token for a gamified reward reveal.
- The reward is between 0.5% and 2% of eligible daily spending, returned as NeuCoins.
- The maximum eligible spend is ₹700 per day.
- The proposed average reward rate is approximately 0.84%.
- There is also a rare chance of winning a Starbucks reward, Croma voucher, or a larger NeuCoins prize.
- The estimated reward cost is approximately ₹52 per active student per month under the assumptions in the document.

**Important:** The exact game interaction has not been finalized. You may visually illustrate a polished reward-reveal concept, but mark it as an illustrative UI concept rather than presenting it as a finalized game mechanic.

The strategic insight is that students already make small payments throughout the day. We want Tata Neu to become their preferred payment app and then create an additional nighttime opening that requires no purchase.

This last point is absolutely essential.

The brief wants students to open the app even when they aren't buying anything.

The nighttime opening is what converts transaction frequency into genuine engagement.

Do not frame this feature as encouraging students to spend additional money to win rewards.
