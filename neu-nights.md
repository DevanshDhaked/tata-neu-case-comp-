# Neu Nights — Feature Proposal

*Tata Neu · Youth & Gen Z segment · Draft v1, 8 Oct 2026*

> Status: everything in sections 1–5 was agreed in discussion unless marked **(suggestion)** or **(open)**. The game itself, meaning what happens on screen at 9 pm, is deliberately left for later.

---

## In one line

**Pay with Tata Neu UPI during the day → 3 small payments unlock a token → between 9 pm and 12 am, play Neu Nights → win NeuCoins back on what you spent that day, plus a rare chance at a big prize.**

| Key number | Value |
|---|---|
| Payments to unlock the night's token | 3 shop payments of ₹10+ |
| Game window | 9 pm – 12 am |
| Spend that counts per day | up to ₹700 |
| Everyday reward | 0.5% – 2% back as NeuCoins (2% is rare), about **0.84% on average** |
| Rare prizes | Starbucks drink, Croma voucher, or up to 2,000 NeuCoins |
| Cost to Tata per active student | about **₹52 a month** |
| Primary success metric | **% of student users who open Tata Neu on 3+ days a week** |

---

## 1. The problem

### What Tata Neu is today

- When you open Tata Neu now, it looks and works like **a payments app, similar to GPay or PhonePe**. Tata has officially refocused it on financial services and loyalty, and wants **10x more people paying through Neu every month**.
- Shopping is mostly a front door to other apps. Fashion, jewellery and groceries **redirect** to Tata CLiQ, Tanishq and BigBasket. Only electronics (Croma), travel and finance live inside Neu.
- So for a 20-year-old, Neu isn't competing with BigBasket or Croma. **It's competing with GPay and PhonePe for the daily payment.** It loses there because it's slower and gives students no reason to switch.

### Why it fails a 20-year-old (the diagnosis)

- **It feels too serious.** The home screen leads with credit cards, personal loans, FDs, bonds and insurance.
- **The rewards don't reach students.** The big NeuCoins earning comes from the NeuCard credit card, which needs age 21+ and income proof. A student's everyday spending, ₹15 chai or an ₹80 canteen meal, earns nothing that feels meaningful.
- **There's no reason to come back between big purchases.** Students open Neu to buy a phone in a sale or book a flight home, then the app sits idle for months ("Buy and Bye").

### The core gap

> **The reward is real, but students can't earn it often enough to care. Nothing in Neu turns the dozens of small payments a student makes every month into a reason to open the app.**

## 2. The feature: Neu Nights

### 2.1 How it works

1. **During the day**, the student pays as usual (chai, canteen, recharge) but uses **Tata Neu UPI** instead of GPay or PhonePe.
2. **After 3 qualifying payments**, they unlock that night's **token**.
3. **Between 9 pm and 12 am**, the Neu Nights game opens. The token lets them play once.
4. The game reveals their reward rate for the day, between **0.5% and 2%**. That rate is applied to the day's qualifying spend, up to ₹700.
5. The reward lands as **NeuCoins** (1 NeuCoin = ₹1), spendable at Tata brands.
6. Every play also carries a **small chance of a rare prize** (section 2.4).

*The game itself (spin, scratch, reveal, or something else) will be designed later.*

### 2.2 Rules

| Rule | Setting | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Payment method | Tata Neu UPI only | Builds the daily payment habit Tata is betting on |
| What counts | Shop (merchant) payments of **₹10 or more** | ₹10 includes campus chai and snacks, the most common student payments |
| Also counts | Mobile recharges and similar regular student payments (e.g. metro card top-ups **(suggestion)**) | Frequent, predictable student spending |
| Doesn't count | Transfers to friends, bank account transfers | Stops people sending ₹10 back and forth to unlock tokens |
| Token | 3 qualifying payments in a day = 1 token | Rewards how *often* students pay, not how much |
| Game window | 9 pm – 12 am | A nightly appointment that lands when hostel students are winding down |
| Spend that counts | Up to **₹700** per day | Caps cost; one big payment can't produce a big payout |
| Reward rate | 0.5% – 2%, with 2% rare | Keeps the average below Tata's own credit card rates |
| Reward currency | NeuCoins | Spendable only at Tata brands, which brings students back into the ecosystem |

### 2.3 Everyday reward: the math

Example spread of results (Tata can tune the odds):

| Result | How often | On a ₹200 day | On a ₹700 day (cap) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.5% | 50% of nights | ₹1.00 | ₹3.50 |
| 1% | 35% | ₹2.00 | ₹7.00 |
| 1.5% | 12% | ₹3.00 | ₹10.50 |
| 2% | 3% | ₹4.00 | ₹14.00 |
| **Average** | | **₹1.68** | **₹5.88** |

**Average rate: 0.84%.** That's below what Tata already pays its own credit card holders on Neu UPI (1% on NeuCard Plus, 1.5% on Infinity). So a student without a card never out-earns a paying card customer.

Over a month, at about ₹200 a day and 20 token nights, a student earns **about ₹34 in NeuCoins** from everyday rewards alone.

### 2.4 Rare prizes: the jackpot layer

On ₹200 a day, the everyday reward is only a few rupees, which won't feel like winning on its own. The rare-prize layer adds the excitement without adding much cost.

| Prize | Odds per play | Cost per player per night |
|---|---|---|
| Starbucks drink (~₹250) | 1 in 500 | ₹0.50 |
| 2,000 NeuCoins **or** ₹2,000 Croma voucher | 1 in 5,000 | ₹0.40 |
| **Total** | | **≈ ₹0.90** |

Most players win nothing extra on most nights. Spread across everyone who plays, the prizes cost Tata **under ₹1 per player per night**.

**What it looks like on one campus:** 500 students × 20 nights = 10,000 plays a month. That's roughly **20 free Starbucks drinks and 2 big prizes every month**. Any one student has about a **1-in-25 chance** of winning a drink in a month. That's rare enough to feel special and common enough that everyone knows someone who won.

**Who pays for the prizes:**
- **NeuCoins top prize:** funded by Tata Digital. The winner has to come back into Neu to spend it, which means more app opens.
- **Starbucks and Croma coupons:** can be co-funded by the brands as their own marketing. A free drink costs Starbucks far less than its menu price and brings a student into the store. A Croma voucher often leads to a bigger purchase.

**Budget control option:** instead of fixed odds, set a fixed number of prizes per night (say, 20 drinks and 2 big prizes) and hand them out randomly. The budget then can't be exceeded, however many people play.

**(suggestion)** Add a middle tier, such as 500 NeuCoins. Odds to be set, since it adds cost.

**(open)** This is a free-to-play promotion: students never pay to enter, and the token comes from normal payments. That's the same model as the scratch cards GPay and PhonePe already run. It still needs a T&C and legal review before launch.

### 2.5 Cost to Tata

Assumptions: a student spends **about ₹200 a day** on qualifying payments and earns a token on **20 nights a month**.

| | Per student per month | Per student per year |
|---|---|---|
| Everyday rewards | ₹33.60 | ₹403 |
| Rare prizes | ₹18.00 | ₹216 |
| **Total** | **≈ ₹52** | **≈ ₹619** |

| Students playing | Cost per year (face value) |
|---|---|
| 1 lakh | ≈ ₹6.2 Cr |
| 10 lakh | ≈ ₹62 Cr |

**Real cost is lower than face value:**
- Some NeuCoins expire unused (they're valid for 12 months).
- Coins are spent at Tata brands, so Tata pays its cost of goods rather than ₹1 per coin. Students often add their own money on top.
- Brand-funded coupons come out of Starbucks' and Croma's marketing budgets, not Tata Digital's.

For scale: Tata Digital reported a ₹4,974 Cr loss in FY26.

### 2.6 What Tata gets back

- **No fee income from these payments.** From 15 Oct 2026, UPI merchant payments above ₹2,000 carry a 0.4% merchant fee, but payments of ₹2,000 or less stay free. Student payments like chai, canteen meals and recharges are far below ₹2,000, so they stay free, and every NeuCoin is a marketing cost. That's why the return has to come from what follows.
- **More people paying through Neu every month**, which is Tata's stated 10x goal for Neu.
- **Coins spent at Tata brands** (BigBasket, Croma, Westside and others), which brings sales into the group.
- **A daily reason to open Neu**, which also exposes students to Croma, travel and other Tata offers they'd otherwise never see.

### 2.7 Guardrails against abuse

- **₹10 minimum and shop payments only** block people from looping tiny transfers between friends.
- **₹700 daily cap** stops one large payment, like rent, from producing a large payout. It also avoids sending a "spend more to win more" message to students.
- **Watch for repeated payments to the same shop** (possible collusion with a merchant), tracked as a guardrail metric (section 5).

## 3. The student's day with Neu Nights

*Example: a 20-year-old hostel student.*

| Time | What happens | Opens Neu? |
|---|---|---|
| 9 am | Pays ₹15 for chai at the campus stall with Neu UPI. **1 of 3** | Yes (payment) |
| 1 pm | Pays ₹80 at the canteen. **2 of 3** | Yes (payment) |
| 6 pm | Pays ₹105 for snacks. **3 of 3.** Notification: *"Token unlocked — Neu Nights opens at 9."* | Yes (payment) |
| 9:30 pm | Back in the hostel, opens Neu and plays. Wins 1% on ₹200 = **₹2 in NeuCoins**. Sees that someone on campus won a Starbucks drink last night. | **Yes, with no purchase** |
| Weekend | Spends a few weeks of coins on BigBasket snacks. Keeps saving toward Croma earbuds. | Yes |

On any day with three small payments, the student opens Neu several times. One of those opens, at night, has nothing to do with buying. Three such days a week meets the success metric.

## 4. Getting students in: marketing and launch

The plan is **advertising plus Instagram**, taking cues from how CRED and slice won young users.

### What CRED and slice actually did

**CRED**
- Launched in **2018 as invite-only**, open only to people with a **credit score of 750+**. Its waitlist reportedly grew into the hundreds of thousands within months. Exclusivity made people want in.
- Built its brand on **big, absurd, celebrity-led ads timed to the IPL**. Rahul Dravid's *"Indiranagar ka gunda"* ad (April 2021) drew **about 3.6 million YouTube views and 114K engagements in its first week**. It became the top trending hashtag and spawned memes and even a spoof from a competitor.

**slice**
- Started in **November 2015 as "Buddy," a credit product for college students**.
- By **August 2016** it had **100 student "college ambassadors" across 80+ colleges**. These were students who worked as interns and growth hackers to bring in users on their own campuses. It had extended credit lines to 6,000 students.
- Later it leaned on **word of mouth**. Its CEO said in 2021: *"Our existing customers tell their friends about Slice."*

### What Neu Nights can borrow (proposals)

- **Launch around the IPL.** Tata is the IPL title sponsor, so ad slots and brand presence are already there. The 9 pm – 12 am window overlaps evening matches, which gives a natural "play during the match" hook.
- **Roll out campus by campus (CRED's exclusivity).** "Neu Nights is now live at IIIT Hyderabad" creates fear of missing out on campuses where it hasn't launched yet. The rollout doubles as the pilot for measuring results (section 5).
- **Campus ambassadors (slice's model).** Students on each campus run the launch, put up stalls near canteens, and share invite codes.
- **Instagram as the main channel.** Post "who won tonight" reels, campus leaderboards and meme-style ads in a Gen Z voice. CRED's humour is the reference point.
- **(suggestion)** **A free first-night token**, so a new student can play once before switching from GPay. That lowers the hardest step: changing UPI apps.

## 5. Success metric

### Primary metric

> **Weekly habit rate: the share of student users who open Tata Neu on 3 or more days in a week.**

**Why not Weekly Active Users (WAU)?** WAU counts a student who opens Neu once to buy a phone in a sale, which is exactly the "Buy and Bye" user. WAU could rise while the problem stays the same. The weekly habit rate:
- matches the brief's goal of **2–3 opens a week**;
- counts **days, not opens**, so it can't be inflated by opening the app repeatedly in one evening;
- captures both the daytime payment opens and the nighttime Neu Nights opens.

### Supporting metrics (why the primary metric moved)

| Metric | What it tells us |
|---|---|
| **First-week activation:** share of new students who earn their first token within 7 days | Whether students actually make the switch from GPay/PhonePe, the riskiest step |
| **Nights played per week** per player | How strong the nightly habit is |
| **Week-4 retention:** share of players still playing 4 weeks later | Whether the habit outlasts the novelty |
| **Neu UPI payments per student per week** | Progress toward Tata's 10x payments goal |
| **Coin redemption at Tata brands** | Whether coins turn into BigBasket, Croma and Starbucks purchases |

### Guardrails (must not get worse)

- **Cost per habit-forming student** stays within about **₹52 a month**.
- **UPI payment success rate and app load time** hold steady or improve. Pushing students into a flow that fails at the counter would backfire.
- **Gaming of the system** stays low, for example clusters of ₹10 payments to the same shop.

### Target **(open)**

Tata doesn't publish how many students use Neu weekly, so the starting point has to be assumed. Two ways to frame the target:
1. **Relative lift**, like the brief's example: *"Double the weekly habit rate among student users within 3 months."*
2. **Campus pilot:** launch on a set of campuses, compare against similar campuses without Neu Nights, and set an absolute goal such as *"X% of players play on 3+ nights a week by week 4."*
