# Neu Nights — Marketing & Launch Plan

*Tata Neu · Youth & Gen Z segment · Draft v1, 9 Oct 2026 · Companion to `neu-nights.md`*

> Everything here is a proposal. Numbers marked **(example)** are starting points to size later, not decisions.

---

## The idea in one line

**Tata doesn't need to buy students' attention. It already owns the stores they walk into, the grocery app they order from, and the IPL title sponsorship. The plan puts the Neu Nights message exactly where a student is about to pay.**

Why not copy CRED or slice? CRED was built for people with credit scores of 750+, and slice was selling a credit card. Neither audience nor product matches a 20-year-old paying ₹15 for chai. Tata's real advantage is the places and moments it already owns.

## 1. "Earn ₹X" on the card machine at Tata stores

### How it works

At most Indian store counters, the card machine shows a UPI QR code for the exact bill amount. Because the machine already knows the bill, it can work out the customer's NeuCoins live (bill × rate) and show it in the space above and below the QR.

**Before paying (QR screen)**
- Top: *Pay with Tata Neu UPI → earn ₹48 in NeuCoins*
- Bottom: *+ counts toward tonight's Neu Nights*

**After paying ("payment successful" screen)**
- Paid with Tata Neu: *You earned ₹48 in NeuCoins 🎉*
- Paid with another app: *You missed ₹48 in NeuCoins. Pay with Tata Neu next time.*

The "you missed" screen is the strongest moment. The student has just paid with GPay and sees the exact amount they left behind. People feel a loss more sharply than an equal gain, which is what changes the next payment.

**Same message on the receipt and SMS bill:** *"You could have earned ₹48 in NeuCoins on this purchase with Tata Neu."*

### Which stores first

| Store | Why |
|---|---|
| **Zudio** | Tata's most Gen Z store: affordable and full of college students. Lead with this one. |
| **Westside** | Regular fashion stop for students |
| **Croma** | Gadgets and accessories; also feeds idea #7 |
| **Starbucks** | A treat rather than a weekly stop, but high visibility |

### Why it's worth it

- **Almost free to run.** Tata controls the counters at its own stores, and many of these machines already show bank offers and EMI options on screen, so this should be a software change rather than new hardware.
- **Reaches every customer, not just students.** That helps Tata's goal of 10x more people paying through Neu every month.

### To confirm before launch
- **The real rate.** The ₹X must match what a customer *without* the NeuCard actually earns paying with Neu UPI at that store. Some stores also give NeuCoins just for sharing a phone number at the counter, whatever app is used. If so, lead with the Neu Nights benefit, which only comes through Neu UPI, so the claim stays true.
- **Detecting the app used.** The "you missed" screen needs the machine to know which UPI app paid. The payer's UPI ID usually shows this; Tata's payments team would need to confirm.
- **Store participation.** Zudio doesn't currently sit inside the Tata Neu app, so its NeuCoins earning would need to be set up.

## 2. BigBasket: "Save more when you pay with Tata Neu UPI"

Groceries and snacks are the most frequent purchase Tata owns, and hostel students order snacks often. That makes BigBasket the best place to build the Neu UPI habit outside physical stores.

### Where to put the message

- **Checkout payment page:** a banner above the payment options: *"Pay with Tata Neu UPI: save ₹25 + counts toward tonight's Neu Nights."*
- **Order confirmation screen:** *"You earned ₹X in NeuCoins"* or *"You could have saved ₹X with Tata Neu UPI."*
- **Delivery bags and inserts:** a small card with a QR to set up Tata Neu UPI and claim a free first token.

### Neu UPI–only coupons

- **(example)** *₹25 off on BB Now orders of ₹199+ when you pay with Tata Neu UPI, for your first 3 orders.*
- **(example)** BigBasket coupons as part of the Neu Nights prize pool, so some nights a student wins *"₹50 off your next snack order."* This keeps the reward inside the Neu app while sending the purchase to BigBasket.

**Why this also helps BigBasket:** its quick-delivery arm, BB Now, is a small player next to Blinkit, Zepto and Instamart. A payment-linked coupon gives students a reason to pick BB Now for that late-night snack order.

## 3. Campus canteens and chai stalls

This is where students make their 3 daily payments, so the message has to be here too.

- **A small standee next to the vendor's existing QR:** *"Pay with Tata Neu UPI here — counts toward tonight's Neu Nights token."*
- **No new hardware.** Any shop's UPI QR already works with the Tata Neu app.
- **(example)** **A small reward for vendors** who display the standee, such as a monthly payout based on the number of Neu UPI payments they receive.
- **A small student team on each campus** places standees, runs the fest stall (#5) and helps friends set up Neu UPI.

---

## 4. Invite a friend

Hostels run on tight friend groups. If one student plays Neu Nights, their room and their group are the next easiest people to bring in.

**How it works**
1. A student shares an invite link from the Neu Nights screen (WhatsApp, Instagram DMs).
2. The friend installs Tata Neu and sets up Neu UPI.
3. **When the friend earns their first token** (3 qualifying payments), both get the reward.

**(example) Reward:** 25 NeuCoins each plus one free token.

**Guardrails**
- Pay the reward only when the friend makes real payments, not on sign-up. This blocks fake accounts.
- **(example)** Cap at 10 successful invites per student per month.

**Cost check:** about ₹50 in NeuCoins for each new active student, close to one month of Neu Nights running cost (~₹52). That's a reasonable price for a student who has already made their first 3 Neu UPI payments.

## 5. College fest "Neu Zone"

Tata brands already sponsor college fests. During a fest weekend, a whole campus is in one place, spending on food, merch and events. That's the best chance to get hundreds of students to try Neu UPI at once.

**How it works**
- **The fest's food and merch stalls become a "Neu Zone."** Their QR codes are registered for the fest days, which is more reliable than using location.
- **(example)** Payments at Neu Zone stalls with Tata Neu UPI earn **double the usual reward**: 1%–4% instead of 0.5%–2%, with the daily cap staying at ₹700. The best possible fest day is ₹28 in NeuCoins.
- **Fest edition of Neu Nights:** extra prizes for that campus that weekend (Starbucks drinks, Croma vouchers). Winners are announced at the stall or on the fest stage, so everyone sees someone win.
- **A Neu stall at the fest** helps students set up Neu UPI on the spot and gives a free first token. This removes the hardest step, switching from GPay.

**When:** fest season. Many campuses hold their fests between October and March.

## 6. IPL season

Tata holds the **IPL title sponsorship from 2024 to 2028** (a ₹2,500 Cr deal), so it already has presence on every broadcast and in every stadium.

**The timing fits Neu Nights.** In IPL 2026, **58 of the 70 league matches started at 7:30 pm**, so the second innings runs right into the 9 pm–12 am Neu Nights window.

**Ideas**
- **Broadcast and stadium ads** using Tata's title-sponsor slots: *"Neu Nights opens at 9. Play during the second innings."*
- **(example) Match-night bonus:** on evening match days, an extra prize pool with IPL match tickets or team merch, things students genuinely want.
- **National launch moment:** after the campus pilot, use IPL to go nationwide.

**Limit:** the IPL lasts about two months (late March to late May in recent seasons). It's the launch push, not the engine. The year-round engine is #1–#4.

---

## 7. Croma big purchase → a free week of Neu Nights

The brief's core problem is "Buy and Bye": students open Neu for a big purchase, then leave. This idea turns that exact moment into the start of the daily habit.

- When a student buys a phone, laptop or earbuds from Croma through Tata Neu, they get **(example) 7 days of free Neu Nights tokens**, with no 3-payment requirement.
- They play every night for a week, see what it's like, and are nudged to keep going with Neu UPI.
- **When:** before the new academic year, when students buy laptops and phones for college.

## How we'll know which channel works

Every channel gets its own entry point (store screen, BigBasket coupon, canteen standee code, invite link, fest Neu Zone, IPL campaign, Croma offer), so each new student can be traced to where they came from.

| Track per channel | Why |
|---|---|
| New students who earn their first token within 7 days | Did the channel get them to actually switch to Neu UPI? |
| Of those, the share opening Neu on **3+ days a week by week 4** | Ties each channel to the primary success metric |
| NeuCoins and coupon cost per active student | Which channel gives habits at the lowest cost |
